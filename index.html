Our story

ArtiBytte started with a simple observation: businesses are becoming more complex, but the technology supporting them is often still disconnected.

Different teams use different tools. Data lives in different places. Processes are repeated. And as a business grows, technology can become something you work around instead of something that works for you.

We believe it can be different.

ArtiBytte was built to create specialized software systems designed around how businesses actually work — not forcing every business into the same software, but giving each one the right experience on top of a shared, intelligent foundation.

That idea became our starting point:

One foundation. Many businesses.

From sports and retail to food, media, AI, and the industries still ahead, our approach is to build systems that understand the workflows they serve while sharing the intelligence, infrastructure, and technology that make them stronger.

We don't believe businesses should have to choose between software that fits their industry and technology that can scale with them.

So we build both.

ArtiBytte designs, builds, and stands behind the systems businesses depend on — connecting people, data, workflows, and intelligence into one foundation built for what comes next.

We're not just building software.

We're building the systems behind the next generation of businesses.

Founder & perspective

Building what I believe businesses will need next.

I started ArtiBytte with a simple belief:

Technology should adapt to the business — not the other way around.

As I worked on different ideas, products, and real business problems, I kept seeing the same pattern. Businesses had the ambition to grow, but the systems supporting them were often fragmented. One tool handled one task, another handled something else, and the connections between them were left for people to figure out.

I saw an opportunity to build something different.

Instead of creating software as isolated products, I wanted to build a foundation that could support many specialized businesses — giving each one its own experience while allowing them to benefit from shared intelligence, infrastructure, and technology.

That became the thinking behind ArtiBytte.

I don't see software as something a business simply buys and uses. It should become part of how the business thinks, operates, and grows.

That means building with the real world in mind: the people using the system, the decisions they make, the data they create, and the problems they need solved every day.

We're still building toward that vision.

But the direction is clear:

Build systems that matter.

Connect what should be connected.

Create technology that becomes stronger as businesses grow.

That's the perspective behind ArtiBytte.

— Founder, ArtiBytte

ArtiBytte ecosystem

Specialized systems. One foundation.

Explore the systems ArtiBytte is building — each designed around a specific real-world workflow, industry, or experience.

Every system in the ecosystem starts from the same question: how does this business, or this person, actually work day to day? The answer shapes the screens, the data, and the automation. Nothing is forced into a generic template, and nothing is built in isolation from the rest.

ArtiPlay — Sports academies

A connected system for running a sports academy. Player rosters, attendance, coach assignments and dues live in one organized environment instead of paper sign-in sheets and scattered spreadsheets. ByteSense, the AI layer, flags no-shows, overdue payments and skill gaps before anyone has to ask. Parents and coaches get check-ins, reminders and progress updates on their phones.

ArtiStock — Inventory & operations

One connected ledger for stock and cost. Receipts, sales, transfers, production runs and counts all land in the same place, so on-hand quantities and true cost stay accurate without spreadsheet reconciliation. It covers weighted-average costing, batches and expiry, purchase orders, recipes and production, multi-location stock, and a full audit trail of who did what.

ArtiSnack — Food & retail POS

A point-of-sale system built around how food and retail businesses actually operate. Every sale syncs to inventory the moment it happens, so stock reflects reality at the counter. It supports digital payments such as GCash and Maya, consolidates multiple branches into one view, and can feed the Business Health Toolkit so the financial picture stays current.

ArtiDrive — Vehicle care & maintenance

A vehicle companion that keeps health, maintenance, fuel and expenses for every vehicle in one app. Battery, oil, tires, brakes and coolant roll into a live health score, with anything that needs attention flagged. Reminders cover oil changes, registration and insurance. You can scan the OR/CR, the odometer or a receipt to fill in records automatically. Gold, the built-in AI, answers questions in plain language.

ArtiHome — Home care & maintenance

The same idea applied to the home. A Home Care Score covers aircon, electrical, plumbing, roof and fire safety. Service reminders arrive before something is due, and expenses and utilities are tracked over time so trends like rising electricity show up early. Titles, insurance, warranties and receipts sit in a document vault, and several properties can live under one account.

Business Health Toolkit

Business intelligence built around one honest number from every source you have. It reconciles data from multiple systems, produces share-ready dashboards for investors and owners, automates recurring financial reports, and looks ahead with cash flow forecasting, so decisions rest on one trusted view.

VEX AI — Automation

The intelligence layer for work that shouldn't need a person. It extracts data from documents, photos and supplier files, automates rule-based workflows, connects AI models into products, and generates custom reports. The same engine powers scanning and import features across other ArtiBytte systems.

ArtiField — Construction control

A control tower for construction projects. Schedule, cost and materials sit in one decision engine: a consolidated Gantt with baseline comparison, S-curves for planned versus actual, cost tracking with CPI and forecast at completion, and stock measured against lead time. Sequence and same-floor clashes are caught before trades collide. Site teams log progress from the field, and each entry is checked against the plan.

OWLBOUND — Games & engagement

An endless night-sky flyer built for engagement. It is one tap to fly, with every run scored, a day and night cycle, power-ups, and a coin and gem economy for revives, skins and trails. Milestones, daily rewards and a leaderboard give players reasons to come back, and progress follows them once they save it with an email.

ArtiSlide — Interactive presentations

A move from static decks to interactive, story-driven presentations. Each slide reveals itself step by step, details open on tap, animated flows show how tools connect, and sliders turn the viewer's own numbers into a live impact estimate. The pitch ends in a lead form that reaches your team directly. It works on phone and desktop.

ATH•NA — Digital identity cards

A personalized digital card with a scannable key at its center. Name, position and brand sit on a front and back that flips with a tap, and one scan opens the card. People can save the contact or book a meeting right from it. Every card gets a unique ATH ID and a sequence number, and it is logged to a registry with its link.
